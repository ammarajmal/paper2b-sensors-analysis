Inputs: camera per-frame channels (S4, S4b; outputs/s4_pose_channels_by_frame.parquet); laser channels from raw (S5a, bridgedeck.laser: heave = centre head, pitch = (side - centre)/dside, bending_avg, torsion_proxy = full difference x dp); reference lines f_h = 1.4318 Hz, f_alpha = 3.0982 Hz; wind speed per RPM from the 2025 laser workbook (measured, tunnel).
Quantities (amplitude of the least-squares sinusoid at the line, search +/- 0.08 Hz, SAME function for camera and laser; broadband RMS stored as well):
 Q1 heave amplitude at f_h: camera = P1 vertical (also P2, P6, H1; cam3 alone; mean of the cameras available) in mm; laser = centre head in mm.
 Q2 pitch amplitude at f_alpha: camera = in-plane roll P5 (also H2) in rad, mean of the cameras available (cam3 alone at 320 RPM); laser = pitch in rad.
 Q3 legacy bending: camera ((y1 + y2)/2 + y3)/2, laser bending_avg; line amplitude at f_h and RMS.
 Q4 legacy torsion proxy: camera y3 - (y1 + y2)/2, laser torsion_proxy; line amplitude at f_alpha and RMS. Camera series are paired by nearest stamp within 0.75 of the median frame interval, no interpolation (SVR rule), on the cam3 stamps.
Pairing arms: A (primary) same nominal RPM: 2025 laser has 20 RPM values 20-320 (20 pairs; 19 with 320 shown separately), 2024 laser has 19 exact matches (20-300). B (secondary) same wind speed: the camera wind speed at each RPM is ASSUMED to be the 2025 workbook relation; the laser amplitude of each session is linearly interpolated in wind speed between neighbouring files, only inside the laser's wind range. For the 2025 laser arm B is identical to arm A by construction; it is informative for 2024 only.
Regime rules (all four computed; every table is produced under each rule and labelled):
 (a) noise: the line amplitude of BOTH instruments >= 5 x their own zero-wind amplitude at that line (camera: e0_0rpm per camera averaged; laser: the bias record D00 / D0 processed with the same function).
 (b) spectrum: the dominant peak of the relevant channel of BOTH instruments lies within +/- 0.10 Hz of the line (camera: S3 refined peak; laser: dominant frequency of bending_avg for Q1, of torsion_diff for Q2).
 (c) wind band: conditions whose wind speed lies between the lowest and highest wind speed at which the 2025 laser line amplitude is >= 5 x its zero-wind amplitude (a band set by one instrument alone), applied to both sessions through their measured wind speeds.
 (d) none: all matched conditions.
Metrics (all stored, per arm x rule x quantity x session): n; Pearson r on linear and on log10 amplitudes; Spearman rho; median camera/laser ratio with a bootstrap 95% interval; typical ratio error E = sqrt(mean(log10(ratio)^2)) (PRIMARY); geometric standard deviation of the ratio; IQR of the ratio; Deming slope (variance ratio 1) with interval; Bland-Altman bias and limits on log10. All intervals: bootstrap over conditions, 2,000 resamples, seed fixed.
Primary analysis (fixed now): arm A, rule (d), quantities Q1 and Q2, E.
Closeness test 2025 versus 2024 (fixed now, advisory input to the owner's decision): on the conditions matched in BOTH sessions (20-300 RPM), for each of Q1 and Q2: dE = E_2024 - E_2025 with a paired bootstrap over conditions (2,000 resamples). "2025 is closer" for the quantity if P(dE > 0) >= 0.95 AND Spearman and log-Pearson of 2025 are not lower than 2024 by more than 0.02. 2025 is judged closer overall only if this holds for BOTH Q1 and Q2. The same dE is also reported for every other arm, rule and quantity, descriptively. A negative or inconclusive result means 2024 is NOT dropped on this evidence.
Figures for judging by eye (results/figures/): log-log scatter camera versus laser with the 1:1 line per session and quantity; amplitude versus RPM for both instruments; ratio versus RPM; Bland-Altman.
Sensitivity (reported, not decisive): camera mm amplitudes scaled by +/- 2.5% (tag size); E for Q1 recomputed.
Integrity pass criteria: (1) pair counts as stated above (2025: 20 matched, 2024: 19); (2) every laser amplitude reproduces from S5a code (same table); (3) no NaN in the matched table except where a camera line amplitude is undefined; (4) the e0 zero-wind amplitudes and the five-times thresholds are printed; (5) the bootstrap runs with the fixed seed.
Caveats written in advance: the camera and each laser session are separate recordings; a ratio near one is not accuracy or simultaneity; the camera scale carries 2.5% from the tag size and the unverified 20 mm tag; the laser pitch depends on dside (13 cm, 10 cm), which was not measured here; the laser noise floors of the two sessions differ.
