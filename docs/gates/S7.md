Stage: S7 (uncertainty budget for the primary camera-laser quantities)   Date written: 2026-10-17 (BEFORE any budget calculation)
Scope: the two primary quantities, Q1 heave amplitude at f_h = 1.4318 Hz and Q2 pitch amplitude at f_alpha = 3.0982 Hz, per condition, camera and laser separately, then the camera/laser ratio. GUM style, relative standard uncertainties, Type A (computed from data) and Type B (stated bounds, rectangular: u = bound / sqrt(3)). No total uncertainty is claimed beyond the quantified components; unquantified components are listed.
Camera components (relative; mean over the cameras that see the condition):
 C1 scale (heave only): tag size bound 2.5% -> u = 1.44%. Roll: none (an angle).
 C2 estimator spread: standard deviation across estimators at the same line (heave: P1, P2, P6, H1, P3, P4; pitch: P5, H2), relative to the mean.
 C3 camera-to-camera: sample standard deviation of the per-camera amplitudes divided by sqrt(n cameras).
 C4 timebase: largest relative deviation from the record-stamp amplitude when the header stamps, a uniform 60 Hz interpolation, or the nominal 60 Hz index is used; rectangular, u = deviation / sqrt(3).
 C5 line search: relative half-range of the amplitude for search half-widths 0.03, 0.05, 0.08, 0.15 Hz; rectangular.
 C6 stationarity within the record: relative standard deviation of the amplitude over three consecutive 10 s windows divided by sqrt(3).
 C7 additive noise: zero-wind line amplitude (e0, mean of cameras) / amplitude, one-sided (relative).
 Informational, not summed: repeat recording: 5-second repeat bag amplitude / main-record amplitude at the same condition (4.5 s records cannot resolve the line; reported for strong conditions only).
Laser components (relative):
 L1 spacing d (pitch only): bound 15% -> u = 8.66% (owner decision). Heave: none.
 L2 stationarity: relative standard deviation over consecutive windows (2025: four 5 s windows; 2024: three 10 s windows) divided by sqrt(n windows).
 L3 additive noise: zero-wind line amplitude (bias record) / amplitude.
 L4 line search: as C5.
 Not quantified (no certificate or no second record): laser calibration factor 2.7 cm/V; head misalignment (2024 ch1 residual 4% of ch2 in torsion); beam-spot versus housing spacing; session effect (separate recordings; indicator only: standard deviation of ln(laser 2025 / laser 2024) at the same RPM on rule-a conditions).
Combination: u_C^2 = sum of C1..C7 squared; u_L^2 = sum of L1..L4 squared; u_R^2 = u_C^2 + u_L^2 (independent). Report per condition and for the rule-a set the median u_R; coverage: share of conditions with |ln(camera/laser)| <= 2 u_R (k = 2), for each session, on rule a and on all 19; and the root-mean-square of ln(ratio) against the root-mean-square of u_R (how much of the observed disagreement the quantified components can explain).
Integrity criteria: every component is computed for all 19 conditions (20-300 RPM) or flagged missing with the reason; no NaN in u_R for rule-a conditions; the zero-wind floors reproduce S6; components are stored in results/s7_budget_by_condition.csv; the script is deterministic.
Failure means: nothing about agreement; it would mean the budget cannot be built for a stated reason.
Result (2026-10-17; scripts/run_s7.py; results/s7_budget_by_condition.csv, results/s7_budget_summary.txt). Integrity criteria met: 76 rows (19 conditions x 2 quantities x 2 sessions), no NaN in u_R, laser amplitudes reproduce S6 to 1e-13.
Median relative standard uncertainty, rule a (both instruments >= 5x their own floor), Q1 heave / Q2 pitch:
 camera u_C 0.14-0.15 / 0.23; laser u_L 0.18-0.20 / 0.10; combined u_R 0.24-0.27 / 0.28-0.30.
 Largest components: C6 stationarity (0.13 heave, 0.10 pitch), L2 laser stationarity (0.17-0.19 heave 2025/2024; 0.06 / 0.02 pitch), L1 spacing (0.087 pitch), C1 scale (0.014). Estimator spread C2, camera-to-camera C3, timebase C4, line search C5, noise C7, L3, L4 are all <= 0.02.
Coverage of |ln(camera/laser)| <= 2 u_R: Q1 2025 12/17 (rule a), 13/19 (all); Q1 2024 12/18, 13/19; Q2 2025 11/13, 16/19; Q2 2024 7/13, 12/19.
RMS of ln(ratio) against RMS of u_R (rule a): Q1 2025 1.03 vs 0.25; Q1 2024 1.33 vs 0.28; Q2 2025 0.78 vs 0.32; Q2 2024 1.05 vs 0.39. The quantified components explain only a small part of the observed disagreement; the RMS is dominated by a few conditions with large ratios while most conditions are covered.
Session effect indicator (SD of ln(laser 2025 / laser 2024), rule-a RPM): Q1 0.59 (mean +0.55), Q2 0.59 (mean +0.17). The two laser sessions differ by more than the quantified laser uncertainty (about 0.2), so session-to-session variation is a missing component, larger than the quantified ones.
Caveats found while building it:
 1. L2 uses four 5 s windows (2025) and C6/L2 short windows: a 5 s window has line resolution 0.2 Hz, wider than the +/-0.08 Hz search, so the window amplitudes are poorly resolved and L2 is probably overstated (an upper bound). C6 uses 10 s windows (resolution 0.1 Hz), less affected.
 2. C3 for heave includes the physical difference between the offset marker (cam1, cam2) and the near-axis marker (cam3); it is small here (0.006) but not a pure measurement term.
 3. C2 uses P1, P2, P6, H1 for heave and P5, H2 for pitch; P3 and P4 (named in the gate) are not in the matched table and were not included.
 4. The informational repeat-recording check was not run.
 5. Unquantified components (calibration 2.7 cm/V, head misalignment, beam-spot spacing, unknown session effect) remain outside u_R; no total uncertainty is claimed.
