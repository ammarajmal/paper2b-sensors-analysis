Stage: S9 (outlier analysis, identification, classification and fixes)   Date written: 2026-10-18 (BEFORE any flag is computed)
Owner goal: find and fix the values that make the camera readings inconsistent with the rest and with the 2025 laser. Rule (reviewer advice, adopted): the laser must NOT be used to find or select fixes. Identification uses camera-internal evidence only; the laser enters afterwards to classify and to report, and any fix is applied to all conditions and reported against BOTH lasers, including when it worsens one of them.
Known before this gate (stated so it is not mistaken for discovery): 60, 80 and 300 RPM are the manuscripts' regime-boundary conditions and 20-60 RPM torsion is at the noise floor (S6, S8). The gate does not use these labels to define a flag.
STEP 1 Camera-internal flags per stream x condition (63 streams; the S2c rules and S4 findings are inputs, not recomputed)
 F1 completeness: fraction of valid frames (detected and decision margin >= 30) below 0.95.
 F2 detection quality: stream median decision margin with modified z-score |z| > 3.5 across the 62 admitted streams (Iglewicz-Hoaglin, MAD based).
 F3 same-marker disagreement: ln(A_cam1 / A_cam2) of the P1 line amplitude at the condition's dominant camera frequency with |z| > 3.5 across conditions (cam1 and cam2 view the same marker).
 F4 stationarity: relative spread of the amplitude over three consecutive windows (S7 component C6) with |z| > 3.5 across conditions.
 F5 repeat disagreement: |ln(A_5s / A_main)| above 2 sqrt(u_C^2 + u_5s^2) where u_5s is the 5 s record's own window spread; conditions without a usable 5 s record are not flagged.
 F6 common-mode line: amplitude at 9.1-9.4 Hz relative to the amplitude at the structural line, per stream, above 0.5 (cam1 and cam2).
 F7 noise floor: camera line amplitude below 5 times its own e0 floor (rule a; this is a floor flag, not a defect flag).
 Not used as flags: IPPE ambiguity (88% of cam1 frames are ambiguous, so it cannot discriminate between conditions; in-plane channels are used), cam3 duplicate frames (one per 16 s in every bag, a constant), the laser.
STEP 2 Classification of each condition's camera-versus-laser disagreement (after step 1; here the laser is used)
 type (a) camera measurement defect: at least one of F1-F6 is set for that condition;
 type (b) regime mismatch between non-simultaneous sessions: no F1-F7 flag, both instruments above their floors, and the dominant frequency of the camera and of either laser channel differ by more than 0.10 Hz, or the amplitude ratio between neighbouring RPM changes sign in one session only;
 type (c) noise floor: F7 set for the camera or the laser amplitude below 5 times its floor;
 type (d) unexplained: none of the above. Reported as unexplained; no cause is invented.
STEP 3 Fix candidates, specified now (all are applied to every condition; none is tuned on the laser)
 X1 robust line fit: Huber-IRLS sinusoid fit (k = 1.345 MAD scale) instead of least squares;
 X2 frame rejection: drop frames with decision margin below the stream's 5th percentile or flagged by a Hampel filter (window 11, 3 MAD) before the fit;
 X3 segmented amplitude: median of the amplitudes over three consecutive windows instead of the single full-record fit;
 X4 inverse-variance camera fusion using the S7 per-camera components instead of the equal-weight mean;
 X5 combinations X1+X2 and X1+X2+X4.
 Selection of a fix is by CAMERA-INTERNAL repeatability only: lower cam1/cam2 disagreement (F3 statistic), lower window spread (F4), lower repeat disagreement (F5). The laser is then reported: E, median ratio and the 2025-versus-2024 closeness for each fix, both lasers, all 19 conditions and rule a.
Pass criteria (machinery): F1-F7 computed for all 63 streams and 19 conditions with no missing value; every condition classified exactly once; each fix evaluated on all 19 conditions; two runs identical; the baseline (no fix) reproduces S6 E and median ratio exactly.
Reading rule: a fix counts as an improvement only if it improves the camera-internal repeatability AND does not worsen the laser agreement of either session by more than the S7 u_R; an improvement of the 2025 agreement alone is reported as "possible tuning to the reference" and is not adopted.
Honest limit stated in advance: types (b) and (c) cannot be fixed by any technique (non-simultaneous sessions; amplitudes at the noise floor); they will be reported with their physical reason.
