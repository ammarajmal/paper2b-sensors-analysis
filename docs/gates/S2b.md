Stage: S2b (background-compensation hypothesis, NOTES F2)   Date written: 2026-10-18 (BEFORE any compensation is computed)
Hypothesis under test (F2): the reference code's ECC affine compensation of "stationary background" either does nothing or removes real model motion, because every trackable feature in cam1/cam2 moves with the tag.
Conditions and streams: e0_0rpm (static), e7_90rpm (torsion lock-in, f near 3.1 Hz), e14_200rpm (strong response), cameras cam1, cam2, cam3 = 9 streams, all frames of each record. Signal: centroid_y (pixels) of the S2 detections, so the test is independent of S4 pose choices.
Three treatments per stream: (N) none: the S2 centroid_y; (C) ECC-compensated: per frame, cv2.findTransformECC (MOTION_AFFINE, criteria 100 iterations or 1e-6, gaussFiltSize 5) of the frame against frame 0, both grey (S2 decode), images downscaled by 2 for the fit, with a mask that excludes the tag bounding box of that frame expanded by 20 px on every side (the reference code's rule); the marker centroid is then mapped back through the inverse of the estimated warp; frames where ECC does not converge keep the uncompensated value and are counted; (R) reference placebo: the same ECC fit using a mask that excludes the whole image except a 100 px wide strip at the image border farthest from the tag (a region that should be the most stationary), to see whether any part of the background is stationary.
Quantities: e0: RMS of centroid_y after median removal and linear detrend (px), per camera and treatment. e7, e14: sinusoid-fit amplitude (px) at the dominant line of the stream within the band 0.5-10 Hz found on treatment N (frequency fixed from N, least squares on the stamps), per camera and treatment, plus the amplitude of the same line in the ECC warp's vertical translation parameter (how much of the line the "background" itself carries). Also report the fraction of non-converged frames.
Decision rule (fixed in docs/STAGE_PLAN.md before this gate): compensation (C) is kept only if it lowers the e0 RMS on all three cameras AND leaves the e7 and e14 amplitude within 2% of N on all three cameras. Otherwise it is not ported.
Additional reading rule, written now: if the warp translation carries the same line as the tag (amplitude ratio warp/tag above 0.5 at e7 or e14), that is direct evidence that the "background" moves with the model and the compensation removes real motion, whatever the e0 result.
Pass criteria for the stage (machinery): all 9 x 3 results computed with no missing value; two runs identical; the N treatment reproduces the S3/S4 stream amplitudes (centroid_y line amplitude equals the one recomputed from the S2 table, difference 0).
Not a criterion: that compensation helps or hurts. The outcome is a finding either way.
Result (2026-10-18; scripts/run_s2b.py, scripts/analyse_s2b.py; results/s2b_compensation.csv; all 9 streams, every frame, 0 non-converged ECC fits).
 DECISION RULE: compensation lowers the e0 RMS on all three cameras: YES (cam1 0.084 -> 0.027 px, cam2 0.090 -> 0.026, cam3 0.047 -> 0.018). Leaves e7/e14 amplitude within 2%: NO. At e7 (90 RPM, 3.11 Hz) the amplitude falls by 99.5% (cam1), 96.9% (cam2), 96.2% (cam3); at e14 (200 RPM) by 46% (cam1), 13% (cam2), and rises by 17% (cam3). Therefore compensation is NOT kept and not ported.
 ADDITIONAL READING RULE (warp translation carries the tag's line): the ECC warp's vertical translation carries 0.45-0.47 of the tag amplitude at e7 for cam1 and cam2 and 1.1-5.7 times it elsewhere; the rule triggers (above 0.5) in 4 of 6 dynamic streams. So the image 'background' that ECC locks onto moves with the model: the part of the scene that is not masked (deck, clamp, ruler) is not stationary, exactly as F2 hypothesised.
 The e0 improvement is explained by the same fact: at e0 the warp carries 0.90-1.07 of the tag's amplitude at the 9.28 Hz common-mode line, i.e. that line is a whole-image motion, and compensation removes it (-81%, -81%, -95% of the line amplitude). So the 9.28 Hz line is visible everywhere in the frame (consistent with camera motion or with all visible parts moving together); this test cannot separate the two, and the S2b result does not change the earlier wording ('an unexplained common-mode line').
 Limits: the border-strip placebo (R) was unstable (amplitude change -98% to +200% across streams), so it is not a usable control and is reported only for completeness; ECC fits use half-resolution images and an affine model; the machinery check that the N amplitude equals the S2/S3 amplitude holds by construction (same centroid column), and a second full run was not repeated (about 3 h of compute).
 Meaning: the reference code's background compensation would delete the signal being measured (here 96-99% of the 90 RPM line) and must not be used. The pipeline's choice of no compensation is justified by data.
