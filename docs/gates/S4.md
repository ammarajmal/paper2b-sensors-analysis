Stage: S4 (camera displacement amplitudes by several estimators)   Date written: 2026-10-17 (BEFORE any pose calculation)
Purpose (owner: "try all possible analysis techniques so that we have rich data"; decisions O3 and the laser comparison in S6 need several candidate channels): compute, for every admitted frame, displacement and rotation channels by independent estimators, and record their spread as the pose-estimation component of uncertainty. NO comparison with the laser is made here (that is S6 with its own gate).
Inputs: S2 detections (corners in the order lb, rb, rt, lt; gate S2 passed); intrinsics = K and 14-term distortion stored in the bag camera_info (owner decision 2026-10-16, identical in all 42 wind-tunnel bags); tag side 20.0 mm (ledger section J: 2025 campaign tags are 20 mm; 35 mm was the 2024 session; this is an input assumption to be confirmed by the owner); record time stamps.
Estimators (each yields x, y in mm in the image-parallel plane, plus rotation where defined):
 P0 raw pixels: centroid of the detector (px), no undistortion, no scale.
 P1 fixed-scale: corners undistorted with K, D; mm per pixel = 20 mm / median undistorted side length of the stream; displacement = (centroid - stream median) * scale.
 P2 per-frame scale: as P1 but mm per pixel = 20 mm / side length of THAT frame (accounts for depth change).
 P3 PnP IPPE (both solutions kept, best by reprojection error) with K and D: translation x, y, z (mm) in the camera frame, and rotation vector (small-angle roll, pitch, yaw in rad). The two-solution ambiguity is recorded as the ratio of reprojection errors; a frame is flagged ambiguous if the ratio < 2.
 P4 PnP iterative (Levenberg-Marquardt) seeded with the IPPE best, same outputs (checks the solver dependence).
 P5 in-plane rotation of the tag from its edge vectors (mean over the four edges, radians, unwrapped): a rotation channel that needs no lever arm.
 P6 corner-mean centroid (mean of the four corners) instead of the detector centre, with the P1 scale.
For each estimator and stream: mean, RMS about the median, peak, and the sinusoidal amplitude at f_h = 1.4318 Hz and at f_alpha = 3.0982 Hz (least squares on the irregular stamps, +/- 0.05 Hz search), and the static (e0_0rpm) noise floor.
Pass criteria:
 1. Synthetic recovery: project a 20 mm square with the real K, D at the measured median pose through cv2.projectPoints, add a known sinusoidal translation of 0.5 mm in y and a 0.02 rad roll, and recover them: P1, P2, P3, P4 amplitude error < 1% and P5 roll error < 1% (noise-free); with 0.05 px corner noise, amplitude error < 5%.
 2. Agreement between estimators on the real data (the view is near-orthographic: fx 20,000 to 26,000 px): per stream, ratio of y RMS (P3/P1) between 0.95 and 1.05 for at least 90% of admitted streams (e0 excluded, its RMS is at the noise floor); the streams outside are listed. Same for P2/P1 and P4/P3.
 3. Static noise floor: e0 y RMS in pixels (P0) within a factor of 2 of the earlier values 0.0842 px (cam1), 0.0902 px (cam2), 0.0465 px (cam3) (SVR S05.01); the corresponding mm values are reported.
 4. Ambiguity: report the share of ambiguous frames per camera; if more than 5% of frames of any stream are ambiguous, the PnP z, roll, pitch and yaw channels of that stream are marked unreliable (the in-plane P1/P2 channels stay).
 5. Scale sensitivity (analytic, not a rerun): report d(amplitude)/amplitude = d(side)/side for a 0.5 mm tag-size uncertainty (2.5% for 20 mm).
Failure means: the estimators disagree beyond the tolerance (then the near-orthographic assumption or the intrinsics are in doubt: report, do not tune); or the static floor is off by more than 2x.
