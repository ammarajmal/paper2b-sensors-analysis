Stage: S3 (per-camera spectra on native timestamps)   Date written: 2026-10-16 (BEFORE any S3 run)
Inputs: our S2 detections (outputs/s2_detect/wtt_main/detections_ALL21.parquet; gate S2 passed, equal to the frozen file); published reference peaks: p02_sensors_mdpi_releases/PAPER2_FINAL_AUDIT_2026-10-01/02_SUPPLEMENT/data/S10_ADMITTED_RETAINED_PEAKS_JOINED.csv (peak_rank 1); reference frequencies f_h = 1.4318 Hz and f_alpha = 3.0982 Hz (gate S5a, 2025 free vibration).
Signal and recipe (the paper's): frames with a detected tag whose decision margin >= 30; centroid_y (px); subtract the median, then a linear trend; band 0.5-10 Hz; frequency grid step RI/10 with RI = 1/T (T = record length of the stream); e20_320rpm cam1 and cam2 excluded (rejected in S2c; cam3 is reported separately as diagnostic-only). 60 admitted streams + 1 diagnostic.
Estimators (all on the same signal):
 E0 = the paper's recipe: scipy lombscargle (precenter, normalised), time = bag RECORD stamp (timestamp_s).
 E1 = generalised Lomb-Scargle (astropy, floating mean), record stamps; Baluev false-alarm probability (FAP) of the highest peak.
 E2 = E0 with the driver HEADER stamp instead of the record stamp.
 E3 = linear interpolation onto a uniform 60 Hz grid, then E0; E4 = nominal 60 Hz frame index, stamps ignored.
Peak frequency uncertainty: refine the rank-1 peak by least-squares sinusoid fit (frequency free within +/- RI of the grid peak); 95% interval from a residual block bootstrap (1-s blocks, 200 resamples).
Pass criteria:
 A. Reproduction: |f(E0) - published rank-1 peak| <= 0.5 RI for at least 58 of the 60 streams.
 B. Estimator agreement: |f(E1) - f(E0)| <= 0.5 RI in at least 95% of streams; any other stream is listed with its two spectra's top-3 peaks.
 C. Timebase: median |f(E2) - f(E0)| <= 0.1 RI; the maximum is reported; E3 and E4 shifts are reported per stream (earlier result: interpolation <= 0.11 RI, nominal index up to 1.49 RI on one stream).
 D. Significance: report the number of streams whose rank-1 peak has Baluev FAP < 0.01; check the Baluev FAP against a white-noise permutation FAP (1,000 shuffles of the y values over the time stamps) on six streams (e0 cam1, e5 cam1, e7 cam1, e7 cam3, e14 cam1, e19 cam2); they must agree to within a factor of 10 on the log10 scale, otherwise the analytic FAP is not used.
 E. Chance baseline for "the dominant peak is within 1 RI of f_h or f_alpha": observed count versus the expected count if the peak were uniform over 0.5-10 Hz (probability per stream = 4 RI / 9.5 Hz), with a binomial p-value. Reported at stream level (60) AND at condition level (19 conditions: a condition counts if at least 2 of its 3 cameras do), because the 60 streams are not independent.
 F. Informational: per-camera recovery error of f_h and f_alpha (nearest rank-1..3 peak within 0.3 Hz), to compare with the earlier 1.59% median figure that used 1.4303 / 3.1036 Hz.
Failure means: the published peaks cannot be reproduced from the bags with our code (A), or the choice of estimator or timestamp changes conclusions (B, C); report and stop before S4.
RESULT (2026-10-17; scripts/run_s3.py, src/bridgedeck/spectral.py; per-stream table results/s3_spectra_by_stream.csv; 31 min 30 s): 61 streams = 60 admitted + e20 cam3 diagnostic.
 A PASS: all 60 streams reproduce the published rank-1 peak exactly (maximum difference 0.000 RI, 60/60 below 1e-6).
 B PASS: generalised Lomb-Scargle (astropy) agrees with the paper's recipe within 0.5 RI in 61/61 streams.
 C PASS: header stamps instead of record stamps: median shift 0.0000 RI, maximum 0.100 RI. Interpolation to 60 Hz: median 0, maximum 0.100 RI. Nominal 60 Hz index: median 0, maximum 1.500 RI (e1_20rpm cam1). So the timestamp choice changes the dominant peak by at most a tenth of a resolution bin, except that ignoring the stamps altogether can move one low-amplitude stream by 1.5 bins.
 D PARTLY INFORMATIVE: Baluev FAP < 0.01 in 61/61 streams. The white-noise permutation check (1,000 shuffles, six streams) can only resolve FAP down to 1e-3, and all six gave that floor, so it confirms FAP < 1e-3 but cannot validate the very small analytic values (1e-50 to 1e-250). Also, FAP tests against white noise: even the 0 RPM stream is 'significant' (its 9 Hz line), so significance does not mean structural. FAP therefore does not discriminate between streams and should not be used as a physical filter.
 E PASS (strongly): the dominant peak is within 1 RI of f_h or f_alpha in 23 of 60 streams (expected by chance 0.8; binomial p < 1e-12), and in 7 of 20 conditions with at least 2 of 3 cameras (chance probability 0.001 per condition). 1 RI is about 0.033 Hz, strict given that the torsion line drifts with fan speed (earlier: 3.115 Hz at 90 RPM to 3.065 Hz at 180 RPM) and the references are wind-off values.
 F INFO: nearest top-3 peak within 0.3 Hz of the reference: f_h median error 2.56% (n = 44 streams), f_alpha 1.15% (n = 26). The earlier 1.59% figure used different reference values (1.4303 / 3.1036 Hz), a full-record Welch estimate and all 57 streams, so it is not directly comparable.
 Peak frequency uncertainty: median 95% half-width 0.0033 Hz, maximum 0.0114 Hz (sinusoid refinement + residual block bootstrap, 200 resamples). This is a fit uncertainty on one stream; it does not include timing or session effects.
Meaning: the Sensors-paper peak table is reproduced exactly from the raw bags by our own pipeline; the estimator and timestamp choice are not what drives the peaks; matches to the structure's frequencies are far above chance at stream and condition level. Selection of what goes in the paper remains open.
