## Folder summaries

- **ai_review**: The ai_review folder contains a collection of documents and scripts aimed at evaluating, revising, and improving the quality of a measurement paper. It focuses on identifying flaws, ensuring reproducibility, and aligning claims with evidence. (role: manuscript; key: CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md, CODE_REPRODUCIBILITY_AUDIT.md, FATAL_FLAW_REGISTER.md, FIGURE_TABLE_AUDIT.md, MEASUREMENT_REVISION_ROADMAP.md)
- **docs**: This folder contains documentation and guides related to the setup of an AI research ecosystem, data acquisition campaigns, and validation methods for the OMRPR Paper 2. It includes recommendations for manuscript updates and comparison strategies between different experimental options. (role: comparison; key: OMRPR_AI_Ecosystem_Setup_Guide.md, option_A_guide.md, option_B_guide.md, verdict.md, option_B_guide.md)
- **docs/legacy_drafts**: This folder contains legacy drafts of a manuscript that are not authoritative for numerical values, timing drift, LDV comparison metrics, and other technical details. It serves as a reference for structure, prose ideas, and sections. It also includes critical reviews and final drafts of the manuscript. (role: manuscript; key: PAPER2_FINAL_DRAFT.md, PAPER2_CRITICAL_REVIEW.md, README.md, PAPER2_FINAL_DRAFT.md, README.md)
- **docs/source_of_truth**: This folder contains documentation and research files related to the OMRPR project, focusing on multi-camera displacement reconstruction, data acquisition, calibration, and validation. It includes manuscripts, results, and pipeline diagrams for ensuring accurate and reproducible research outcomes. (role: acquisition; key: PROJECT_CONTEXT.md, README.md, RESULTS_LOG.md, claim_boundary.md, pipeline_diagram.md)
- **manuscript/literature_review**: This folder contains a literature review focused on vision-based displacement measurement techniques, their validation against reference sensors, and challenges in synchronization, calibration, and accuracy. It covers applications in wind tunnels, bridges, and structural health monitoring. (role: comparison; key: first.md, seventh.md, fourth.md, second.md, sixth.md)
- **notes**: This folder contains documentation and checklists related to the review and revision of the OMRPR Paper 2 manuscript, focusing on measurement accuracy, validation, and governance. It includes notes on risks, comparisons, and synchronization issues in experimental setups. (role: comparison; key: claude_instructions.md, reviewer_risk_checklist.md, reviewer_risk_scan_initial.txt, reviewer_risk_scan_tex_only.txt)
- **scripts**: This folder contains scripts for generating figures, verifying calibration data, and computing statistical measures such as RMSE, MAE, and Bland-Altman analysis to support the manuscript's claims. It also includes utility scripts for building and cleaning the manuscript. (role: comparison; key: bootstrap_condition_level_ci.py, gen_fig03_ldv_scatter.py, gen_fig08_bland_altman.py, verify_option_b.py, rebuild_option_b_corrected.py)
